about me

hi, I'm Prarthana —

a girl who tends to think about things for far longer than necessary.

why this corner exists

because some thoughts don't feel finished when they leave your head.

Sometimes I read something, notice something, hear someone's story, or experience something completely ordinary — and somehow it stays with me. So I started putting those things here.

I'm studying Information Technology, which means I spend a lot of my calendar around code and computers. But out here, I'm mostly into stories, ideas, people, art, and the strange little things that make us human. This place is my way of living a little beyond the code — and maybe, somewhere in the middle, finding a few people I can resonate with, and who can resonate back.

"I don't believe a mind only ever thinks about one thing. Niche-finding is a journey, and not everything has to be sorted or perfect."

what I'll write about

Honestly? Very random things. Whatever shows up on a random day — a thought, a song, a stranger's face, a question my brain refuses to let go — if it stays with me, it earns a seat here. Some posts will be half-finished ideas. That's allowed.

what I hope you feel

Seen. Heard. Maybe that you got a piece of advice right when you needed it. I hope you find things here you can relate to — and I hope you understand that you're not alone in your hidden battles. If ever you want to express yourself too, there's room for that here.

"You're not alone in your hidden battles."

what I hope this becomes

A café. You know the kind — where you sit with a coffee, take slow sips, and just think about your life, or read a book and resonate with the character. I want this corner to be that safe, comforting place for everyone who visits: a seat, a warm drink, and permission to pause.

Take a cup. Stay a while. Wander back whenever.
